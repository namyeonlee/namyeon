I study how health and science messages shape what people understand, believe, and do—especially when those messages appear on social media, short videos, or other new technologies. My work combines research-based theory with real-world applications, often examining how different message elements—such as message framing, visuals, or the credibility of the source—affect audience responses.
I focus on topics that often involve uncertainty, innovation, or sensitive health issues, aiming to create communication strategies that make information clearer, reduce misinformation, and build public trust.
